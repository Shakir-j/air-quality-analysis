Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Summary: AIRWISE does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The platform visualises publicly available government environmental data and runs all computation locally.

1. Who We Are

AIRWISE is an environmental intelligence platform that analyses and visualises air quality records sourced from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) of India. The platform is provided for informational and research purposes.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

AIRWISE does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact details
  • Account credentials or registration information
  • Location data from your device
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising purposes
  • Device fingerprints or persistent identifiers

3. Data Processed Locally

All air quality data displayed by AIRWISE is derived from the CPCB open dataset (2010 to 2023). This data is pre-processed, cached server-side, and served as static JSON responses. No personal data is involved in this process.

Any prediction inputs you enter into the Prediction tool are sent to the local API server for computation and are not logged, stored, or transmitted to any third party.

4. Server Access Logs

Like any web server, the server software (Uvicorn/FastAPI) may generate standard HTTP access logs containing IP addresses, request paths, and timestamps. These logs are retained only as long as necessary for debugging and security and are not shared externally.

5. Third-Party Services

AIRWISE loads all dependencies (Leaflet.js, Plotly.js) from local vendor files. No third-party scripts, analytics services, or advertising networks are loaded by this platform.

6. Data Source Attribution

All environmental records are sourced from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) open data portal under its public data access policy. AIRWISE does not claim ownership of any underlying measurement data.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Material changes will be noted clearly at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the project repository. There is no data rights request process because no personal data is held.
